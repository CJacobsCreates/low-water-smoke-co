Pit notes

Which bottle, which cook?

01 · BRISKET

Brisket Rub, then Original on the side

A tablespoon of rub per pound, 250°F over post oak, wrap in butcher paper around 165°F, pull when it probes like butter (usually 200–205°F). Original goes on the table, not the meat.

02 · PULLED PORK

Sweet Pecan, finished with Carolina Gold

Rub a bone-in shoulder the night before, cook at 250°F to about 203°F, rest an hour, pull, then toss with warm Carolina Gold.

03 · CHICKEN

Yard Bird rub, Alabama White to dunk

Spatchcock, rub, smoke at 325°F until the thigh reads 175°F. Dunk the pieces straight into a bowl of Alabama White while they're hot.

Our story

It started when the creek came up.

In the spring of 2019 rain flooded the crossing at the bottom of our road outside Llano and kept us home with a smoker, a brisket and nothing to do but wait. That batch of sauce got passed around the whole road. By fall we were filling bottles in a rented church kitchen on Tuesday nights.

It's still just the two of us, Wade and Celia, plus a neighbor on labeling nights. When the creek's up we're slow to ship. When it's down, we're cooking.

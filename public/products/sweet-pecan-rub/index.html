Sweet Pecan Pork & Poultry Rub

Brown sugar, paprika and toasted pecan for a mahogany bark.

Ships Monday to Thursday from Texas. Free US shipping on orders $60+.

Our answer to every "what do I put on a pork butt?" text. Dark brown sugar and sweet paprika build a deep mahogany bark, a pinch of chipotle keeps it from being one-note, and finely ground toasted pecan adds a nutty finish you notice on the second bite.

Shake it on heavy, let it sit while the pit comes up to temp, and cook low.

Ingredients & allergens

Dark brown sugar, sweet paprika, kosher salt, black pepper, toasted pecan meal, granulated garlic, chipotle powder, ground mustard.

Contains: tree nuts (pecan), mustard.

Pairs with

Pork shoulder, spare ribs, whole chicken, salmon, roasted sweet potatoes

Size, storage & care

Net Wt 5.5 oz (156 g) shaker tin. Keep the lid on. Brown sugar may clump; shake hard. Best within 9 months.

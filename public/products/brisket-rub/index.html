Brisket Rub: Salt, Pepper & Then Some

16-mesh pepper, kosher salt and a quiet backbone of garlic.

Central Texas brisket is salt and pepper, and we didn't mess with that. We sized the pepper right (16-mesh, the coarse grind pitmasters argue about), balanced it with kosher salt, and added a little granulated garlic and onion so the bark tastes finished.

Use about a tablespoon per pound. It does the same job on beef ribs, a thick ribeye or a pot of chili.

Ingredients & allergens

16-mesh black pepper, kosher salt, granulated garlic, granulated onion.

No sugar, no MSG, no anti-caking agents.

Pairs with

Brisket, beef ribs, tri-tip, ribeyes, smash burgers

Size, storage & care

Net Wt 6 oz (170 g) shaker tin, or 1 lb (454 g) refill bag. Keep the lid on and away from the stove. Best within 12 months.

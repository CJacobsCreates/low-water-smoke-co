Pit notes

Which bottle, which cook?

Read the recipes
01 · BRISKET

Brisket Rub, then Original on the side

A tablespoon of rub per pound, 250°F over post oak, wrap at 165°F. Original goes on the table, not the meat.

02 · RIBS

Sweet Pecan, glazed with Mesquite

Rub heavy, cook 5 hours, then brush on Mesquite Molasses for the last 20 minutes so it tacks up.

03 · CHICKEN

Hot Peach on smoked thighs

Skin-on thighs at 300°F until 175°F inside. Toss in warm Hot Peach and give them two minutes over direct heat.

Our story

It started when the creek came up.

Spring rain flooded the crossing at the bottom of our road and kept us home for a day with a smoker, a brisket and nothing to do but wait. That batch of sauce got passed around the whole road. By fall we were filling bottles in a rented church kitchen on Tuesday nights.

We still cook the same way. When the creek's up we're slow to ship. When it's down, we're cooking.

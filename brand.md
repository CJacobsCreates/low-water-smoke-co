# Low Water Smoke Co. (fictional demo brand)

> Demo brand created by Jacobs Consultancy to show what a Shopify store build looks like. Low Water Smoke Co., its founders and its products are fictional. A web search (21 Oct 2026) turned up no existing BBQ sauce, rub or food brand called "Low Water Smoke".

## Name
**Low Water Smoke Co.** Short form on labels and merch: **LOW WATER**.

A "low-water crossing" is the concrete slab where a Texas back road dips through a creek bed instead of bridging it. Every Texan knows the yellow "Watch for water on road" signs. The name is local, a little humble, and it gives the brand one strong visual: the engraved creek crossing that appears on every label.

## Tagline
**Small-batch sauce from the bottom of the road.**

Alternates for ads and social:
- Thin sauce. Thick bark. No shortcuts.
- Cooked low. Bottled by hand.

## Story (About page, ~120 words)
Low Water started on a Saturday in 2019, when spring rain flooded the crossing at the bottom of our road outside Llano, Texas, and kept the two of us home with a smoker, a brisket and nothing to do but wait. That batch of sauce got passed around the whole road. By fall we were filling bottles in a rented church kitchen on Tuesday nights.

It's still just Wade and Celia, plus a neighbor on labeling nights. We cook in small batches, 40 gallons at a time, with post oak smoke instead of the liquid kind and black pepper ground coarse enough to argue about. Every bottle is filled, capped and labeled by hand. When the creek's up we're slow to ship. When it's down, we're cooking.

Fictional founders for copy only: **Wade and Celia Harlan**.

## Fonts (Google Fonts)
- **Display / headings:** *Zilla Slab* 600/700. A sturdy slab serif that echoes the lettering on the bottle label. Use uppercase with +2% letter-spacing for short headings, sentence case for long ones.
- **Body:** *Libre Franklin* 400/500/600. A plain, warm American grotesque that reads well at 16px on mobile.
- **Details:** *IBM Plex Mono* 500. Used sparingly for heat levels, sizes, batch numbers and SKUs, like a stamped batch code.

All three are in Shopify's built-in font library (checked against shopify.dev's font list: `zilla_slab`, `libre_franklin`, `ibm_plex_mono`), so Dawn's font picker can use Zilla Slab for headings and Libre Franklin for body with no custom code.

## Voice
- **Who's talking:** a pitmaster friend at the end of the table, not a marketing department.
- **Plain and specific:** "16-mesh pepper", "40 gallons at a time" and "post oak" instead of "premium", "artisanal" or "bold flavor explosion".
- **Dry humor, never forced:** one wink per page at most ("a fried egg at 2 a.m.").
- **Confident about Texas BBQ, generous to everyone else:** we never trash other styles or brands.
- **Short sentences.** American spelling, US units first (oz, lb, °F).
- **Avoid:** exclamation-point stacks, emoji, "elevate your grilling game", "taste explosion" and fake scarcity.

## Photography rules
- Studio: weathered limestone slab, warm cream backdrop, soft light from the left, one or two raw ingredients beside the product.
- Lifestyle: real food (sliced brisket with a pepper bark), butcher paper, dark wood, golden-hour Hill Country light.
- Labels always face the camera. Never show people's faces; hands only.
